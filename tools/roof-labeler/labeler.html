Roof Damage Labeler

Open a folder of roof photos to start labeling

Everything stays on your computer. Progress autosaves in this browser, and Export labels gives you the zip to hand back.

  1. Type your name at the top right.
  2. Click Open folder and pick the folder with the photos (.jpg, .png, .webp).
  3. If the folder also has YOLO .txt labels or an annotations.json, those boxes, flags and notes load too.

Follow the labeling guide in tools/roof-labeler/README.md for what to box and when to flag.

    —
    Box: drag on the photo. Shape: click around the damage, then click the first point or press Enter. Click a shape to select it and drag it to move. Drag any corner anywhere, so a box can be skewed to follow the roof's angle. Drag a small diamond to add a corner, double-click a corner to remove it. × or Del deletes the shape, Ctrl+Z undoes, 1 2 3 change its class. Zoom: Ctrl+scroll or pinch zooms where the pointer is, + and − zoom on the selected shape, 0 fits the photo. Scroll, or hold Space and drag, to move around.
