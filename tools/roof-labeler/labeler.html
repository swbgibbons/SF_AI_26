Roof Damage Labeler

Open a folder of roof photos to start labeling

Everything stays on your computer. Progress autosaves in this browser, and Export labels gives you the zip to hand back.

  1. Type your name at the top right.
  2. Click Open folder and pick the folder with the photos (.jpg, .png, .webp).
  3. If the folder also has YOLO .txt labels or a .json annotations file (any name), those shapes, flags and notes load too and you can edit them.
  4. If Open folder won't let you pick, use Open files instead: select all the photos and the .json file (Cmd+A selects everything).

Photos that already have colored boxes printed on them (the previews zips) can't be edited, because those boxes are part of the picture. Open the plain photos with their .json file instead.

Follow the labeling guide in tools/roof-labeler/README.md for what to box and when to flag.

    —
    Box: drag on the photo. Shape: click around the damage, then click the first point or press Enter. Click a shape to select it and drag it to move. Drag any corner anywhere, so a box can be skewed to follow the roof's angle. Drag a small diamond to add a corner, double-click a corner to remove it. × or Delete deletes the shape, Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) undoes, 1 2 3 change its class. On a Mac trackpad, Ctrl-click or two-finger click a corner to remove it.
